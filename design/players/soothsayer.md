# Soothsayer

*Status: draft; every number is a placeholder for playtesting. Structured version: `soothsayer.json`.*

**Identity:** Calm counterweight to loud characters. Controls the storyline with Redirect, foresight and gold-paid cancels. Weak on damage.

## Character card

- **Health:** 8
- **Hand size:** 6 (drawn up to at the start of the player phase)
- **Ability, Thread Reader:** Once per round, add 1 omen to the pool: Flip an arrow in the storyline (Redirect: Flip). Usable in the player phase, or while a storyline card is about to resolve.
- **Name and story:** not written yet (`title` and `story` are empty in the data)

## Design notes

- The four Redirect cards use three different forms (Flip, Swap, Remove) so playtests can decide which forms stay (open question 3).
- Cancels cover Attack, Curse, Hazard and Summon. Story can't be cancelled or removed (placeholder rule).
- Foresight reorders the entity deck, which changes which cards sit next to each other, and so which arrows chain into which split cards, before the reveal.
- The Augur's Purse is the standard gold pouch as a signature card. Deep Purse is its upgrade.
- No kit card. The identity ability is the Seer's starting tool.
