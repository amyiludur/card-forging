# Scenario 3: March of the Dead Legion

*Status: first draft. Every number is a placeholder for playtesting. Structured version, with the full deck, board, beats and town: `data/march-of-dead-legion.json`.*

*Formerly Plague of the Dead Legion. The plague half moved to The Pale Fever and the scenario became a siege.*

**Type:** concept (no health)
**Overview:** Three hundred years ago a legion died holding the barrows above this village. Tonight they are rising, and they have come to lay siege to it. There is no boss to kill. The players hold the village until dawn.

**Starting Dread (X):** 2 + 1 per player
**Dread effect:** set the Well on fire. If it is already burning, set fire to another district.

## The three ideas it is built on

- **The army is patient.** The **March** (0 to 12) only ever goes up. The Barrows add 1 every entity phase, and nothing removes it. Players can only stop it rising, with the Bell Tower holding it for a round. If it reaches 12 the players lose.
- **The mist hides the arrows.** From beat 2, the first card revealed each round is **Shrouded**: face down, arrow and effect hidden. Arrows are what let players plan the storyline, so the Mist takes away the thing they rely on. The Watchfire turns Shrouded cards face up.
- **The village burns.** The Legion sets the town's districts on fire. A burning district can't be used, and the Well puts a fire out, but a district that has been put out can catch fire again. Once 3 are burning the fire spreads by itself, and if every district is burning at once the players lose.

## The fire is meant to overwhelm by the end

Each beat sets more fires than the last, so early on the Well keeps up and late on it falls behind:

| When | Source of fire |
|---|---|
| From the start | *Knocking* and *Called by Name* (top half) |
| Any Dread check that comes up short | The Well, or another district if the Well is already burning |
| Beat 1 advances | *Roll Call* (bottom half) |
| Beat 2 advances | *The Dead Come Home*: set fire to 2 districts |
| Beat 3 | The Standard sets fire to a district every entity phase until it falls |
| Beat 4 | Grey Light sets fire to 2 districts the moment it is reached |
| Any time | Once 3 districts are burning, another catches every entity phase |

## How the night runs

| Beat | Name | Advance | On advance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Barrows Open | Destroy 3 Risen, the March reaches 3, or resolve *The Barrows Stir* | Shuffle in 3 *Roll Call* |
| 2 | Mist on the Fields | The March reaches 6, or destroy 4 Risen | Dread +1. Shuffle in 3 *Grave Muster* and 2 *The Dead Come Home* |
| 3 | The Standard Raised | Destroy The Standard | Dread +1. Shuffle in 3 *One Last Push* |
| 4 | Grey Light | Survive 2 rounds | The players win |

The Legion's own advance moves beats 1 and 2 on, so the night passes either way. What matters is whether the village is still the players' when dawn comes. Each beat makes the deck harsher. Beat 3 is the one objective that has to be won by force.

## Designer notes

- **A siege, not a plague.** This scenario used to carry plague counters as well, a fourth system and a second way for each player to die. They moved to The Pale Fever, which is the plague scenario, along with the Physician and *Grave-Cold Well*.
- **No final boss, on purpose.** The Standard has health, but it is a banner to cut down, not a villain. The win is being outlasted, not a kill.
- **Fire no longer adds to the March.** With districts catching again and the fire spreading, feeding the March as well would end the game twice over. The fire is its own lose condition instead.
- **The Dread effect goes for the Well first.** Losing the Well is losing the way to fight fire, so a short Dread check hurts the defence before it hurts a district.
- **A burning Well can only put itself out, and it costs the player who does it 1 damage.** So the defence is never shut off completely, but getting it back has a price.
- **The March clock still needs checking in playtesting.** Beats 3 and 4 have to fit between March 6 and 12, and the Bell Tower is the only brake.
- **Putting fires out scales with the table and setting them doesn't yet.** Each player can use the Well once a round, so four players put fires out far faster than one. The sources of fire may need to count the players.
- **The first player chooses which district catches fire**, so the choice still hurts but isn't random.
- **The town is four districts now**: Well, Bell Tower, Watchfire, Smithy. The Physician left with the plague.
- **Wording still to do.** Card names and flavour still lean on graves and the dead (*Knocking*, *Called by Name*, *Roll Call*, the chapel bell in beat 1's flavour) rather than a siege. The replacement halves on *Voices in the Mist* (2 damage) and *Roll Call* (add 1 to the March) are placeholders.
- **Modules:** a play still needs 1, and none exists for this scenario yet. Kraken's sea and sky modules don't fit a concept entity.
