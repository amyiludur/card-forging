# Scenario 3: Plague of the Dead Legion

*Status: first draft. Every number is a placeholder for playtesting. Structured version, with the full deck, board, beats and town: `data/plague-of-dead-legion.json`.*

**Type:** concept (no health)
**Overview:** Three hundred years ago a legion died holding the barrows above this village. Tonight they are rising, and a plague walks among the living to bring them home. There is no boss to kill. The players hold the village until dawn.

**Starting Dread (X):** 2 + 1 per player
**Dread effect:** each player gains 1 plague counter.

## The three ideas it is built on

- **The army is patient.** The **March** (0 to 12) only ever goes up. The Barrows add 1 every entity phase, and nothing removes it. Players can only stop it rising, with the Bell Tower holding it for a round. If it reaches 12 the players lose.
- **The mist hides the arrows.** From beat 2, the first card revealed each round is **Shrouded**: face down, arrow and effect hidden. Arrows are what let players plan the storyline, so the Mist takes away the thing they rely on. The Watchfire turns Shrouded cards face up.
- **Every death leaves its mark.** The dead Mark the town's districts. A Marked district can't be used, and the Chapel cleanses it, but a cleansed district can be Marked again. Once 3 are Marked the rot spreads by itself, and if every district is Marked at once the players lose.
- **The plague kills.** If a player's plague counters equal their maximum health, they die. Plague counters come from the Dread effect and from cards, and only the Physician removes them.

## Marking is meant to overwhelm by the end

Each beat adds more marking than the last, so early cleansing keeps up and late cleansing falls behind:

| When | Source of Marks |
|---|---|
| From the start | *Knocking* and *Called by Name* (top half) |
| Beat 1 advances | *Roll Call* (bottom half) |
| Beat 2 advances | *The Dead Come Home*: Mark 2 districts |
| Beat 3 | The Standard Marks a district every entity phase until it falls |
| Beat 4 | Grey Light Marks 2 districts the moment it is reached |
| Any time | Once 3 districts are Marked, another is Marked every entity phase |

## How the night runs

| Beat | Name | Advance | On advance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Barrows Open | Destroy 3 Risen, the March reaches 3, or resolve *The Barrows Stir* | Shuffle in 3 *Roll Call* |
| 2 | Mist on the Fields | The March reaches 6, or destroy 4 Risen | Dread +1. Shuffle in 3 *Grave Muster* and 2 *The Dead Come Home* |
| 3 | The Standard Raised | Destroy The Standard | Dread +1. Shuffle in 3 *One Last Push* |
| 4 | Grey Light | Survive 2 rounds | The players win |

The Legion's own advance moves beats 1 and 2 on, so the night passes either way. What matters is whether the village is still the players' when dawn comes. Each beat makes the deck harsher. Beat 3 is the one objective that has to be won by force.

## Designer notes

- **No final boss, on purpose.** The Standard has health, but it is a banner to cut down, not a villain. The win is being outlasted, not a kill.
- **Marks no longer add to the March.** With districts re-Marked and the rot spreading, feeding the March as well would end the game twice over. The Marks are their own lose condition instead.
- **The March clock still needs checking in playtesting.** Beats 3 and 4 have to fit between March 6 and 12, and the Bell Tower is the only brake.
- **Cleansing scales with the table and marking doesn't yet.** Each player can use the Chapel once a round, so four players cleanse far faster than one. The marking sources may need to count the players.
- **The first player chooses which district is Marked**, so the choice still hurts but isn't random. A Marked Chapel can only cleanse itself, so the rites are never shut off completely, just slowed.
- **Modules:** a play still needs 1, and none exists for this scenario yet. Kraken's sea and sky modules don't fit a concept entity.
