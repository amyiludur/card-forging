# Keywords and Terminology

## Keywords
- **Dread:** the penalty when too few cards are revealed. Its threshold X rises during the game.
- **Redirect:** change an arrow in the storyline, which changes which half of a split card resolves. Character-specific. Candidate forms *(not final)*:
  - **Flip:** choose a storyline card. Its arrow counts as pointing the opposite way until the end of the round. *(Current placeholder default.)*
  - **Swap:** exchange two cards in the storyline, so different arrows precede them.
  - **Remove:** take a card out of the storyline, so the card before it and the card after it become neighbours.
- **Empowered:** the bonus an entity card gets from omen overshoot (+1 effect strength per point of excess).
- **Response:** a player card played while an entity card is about to resolve, paid for with gold. (Only mentioned in passing so far, needs a full definition.)
- **Type:** the general category of an entity card's effect (Attack, Hazard, Summon, Curse, Story).
- **Trait:** a scenario-specific tag on a card, used for targeted effects.

## Terminology
- **Entity:** what the players face. A creature, a concept, or a group.
- **Entity deck:** the deck revealed by the omen system (base deck plus module cards).
- **Entity board:** persistent cards the entity sets up at the start.
- **Story beat:** a key set-aside card that shapes the scenario.
- **Storyline:** the row of revealed entity cards (working name; alternatives: chapter, scene, act, saga).
- **Arrow:** the marker on the right edge of every entity card, pointing at the top or bottom. It decides which half of the next split card resolves.
- **Module:** a themed set of entity cards (and sometimes board cards) that is added to a scenario's entity deck at setup.
- **Omen pool:** the counters players build up.
- **Omen cost:** the value on an entity card.
- **Gold pouch:** a deck feature that stores up to 2 gold between rounds.
- **Town:** the shared place where players heal, buy potions and upgrade cards.
- **Shop:** the set of cards set aside during deck building that players can buy during the game.
